What It Does

☀

Weather Intelligence

Live conditions, next-hour precipitation, 24-hour and 16-day forecasts with a dynamic, condition-aware gradient.

◉

Long-Range Outlook

Real forecasts for ~16 days, then climatological estimates with confidence bands out to a decade ahead.

⚑

Interactive Maps

5 basemaps, 6 weather overlay layers with legends, directional wind arrows, and a GeoRadar 3D terrain mesh.

⚠

Disaster Monitoring

USGS, NASA EONET, and GDACS feeds on an interactive world map — filter by type, date, and natural-language search.

◎

Risk Assessment

A 0–100 risk score blending weather severity, disaster proximity, air quality, and terrain exposure.

⚡

Threshold Alerting

Custom rules across 10 metrics with browser notifications, per-location cooldowns, and local persistence.

▤

PDF & Email Reports

One-click A4 intelligence reports plus daily/weekly email summaries via Web3Forms — no backend needed.

▣

PWA & Offline

Installable with a service worker caching API responses and map tiles for offline use.

◆

Auto-Disaster Alerts

Automatic monitoring for M6+ earthquakes and Cat 3+ cyclones with a top-of-app alert banner.

Why It Matters

  • One platform for weather, climate, disasters, risk, and reporting — no tool-hopping.
  • Zero cost, zero keys — powered entirely by free, CORS-enabled public APIs.
  • Honest forecasting — real model output to ~16 days, then clearly-labeled climatological estimates.
  • Professional output — A4 PDF intelligence reports and scheduled email summaries.
  • Works anywhere — installable PWA with offline caching for field use.

Honest Long-Range Forecasting

Real numerical weather forecasts are only skillful to about 16 days. Days 0–16 use actual high-resolution model output; beyond that, Weatherly shows a climatological estimate — the historical normal from ~12 years of ERA5 reanalysis, optionally blended with CMIP6 projections — drawn as dashed lines with a confidence band that widens with lead time.
